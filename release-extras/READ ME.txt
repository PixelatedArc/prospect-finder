PROSPECT FINDER
===============

Works on any Windows 10/11 PC. Nothing to install, no Node.js, no Claude needed.

SET UP ON A NEW PC
  1. Copy this whole "Prospect Finder App" folder to the PC
     (somewhere it will stay, e.g. Documents).
  2. Double-click "Create Desktop Shortcut.bat".
  3. A "Prospect Finder" icon appears on the desktop. Open it.

  If you move this folder later, run "Create Desktop Shortcut.bat" again.
  If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info > Run anyway.

NOTHING IS STORED ON THE PC
  The app keeps no results, no history and no browser data. Every run goes
  straight to your Google Sheet, and the browsing data is erased after each
  run. The only thing saved is one small settings file (your sheet
  connection, your name and your email template).
  This also means: if the sheet is not connected, results exist only on
  screen. Use "Copy rows" before closing the app.

CONNECT YOUR GOOGLE SHEET AND GMAIL (one time, about 2 minutes)
  Open "Google Sheet and Gmail connection" inside the app and follow the
  5 steps. Always use the "Copy the connector code" button in the app,
  because it puts your private key into the code.

  Do the steps in a browser signed in ONLY as the Google account you send
  outreach from (a private window is easiest). Make a blank sheet at
  sheets.new and give it a name; the app creates all tabs itself.
  Emails are sent from whichever Google account deploys the connector.
  If Deploy says "You do not have permission", you are signed into a
  second Google account or the sheet belongs to another account.

  On your other PCs you do not repeat those steps: on the first PC press
  "Copy connection code", send it to yourself, and paste it into the same
  box on the other PC. Keep that code private.

HOW TO USE
  1. Type the niche (e.g. dentist), pick the country (United States,
     United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, New Zealand or Ireland), and
     start typing the city: every city and town in that country is
     suggested, largest first. Any place you type yourself also works.
     Outside the US a note shows that country's cold-email rules.
  2. Choose how many sites to check (5 to 10) and press Run.
  3. A browser window opens and does the work: searches Google Maps,
     skips the top 3, opens each next website and checks
       - does each service have its own page
       - is there FAQ content
       - does the schema declare the business itself (Dentist, Plumber,
         LocalBusiness...) and how complete it is. Automatic schema from
         site builders (Organization, WebSite) counts as "no".
     A site that fails 2 or 3 of those is marked WEAK.
     It then opens the homepage as a phone would and checks
       - does the site fit a phone screen (Mobile column)
       - is there a tap-to-call button (Tap to Call column)
       - does the phone number on the site match the Google listing
         (Phone vs Google column)
     It also notes the footer agency credit and any email on the site.
     Every business gets a serial number in the "#" column of the sheet.
  4. 8 sites take roughly 5 minutes. Leave the browser window alone
     while it runs. If Google asks "I'm not a robot", tick it in that
     window and the run continues by itself.
  5. The rows are added to your sheet in a tab named with the date
     (e.g. "Nov 16, 2026"). A new day gets a new tab automatically.
     Businesses already anywhere in the sheet are not checked again.

THE WHOLE PROCESS, STEP BY STEP
  1. FIND. Run a niche and city. Every weak site gets a Priority
     (High, Medium, Low) based on its Google reviews, how weak the site
     is, whether a personal email and the owner's name were found, and
     whether an agency already looks after the site.

  2. EMAIL. Under "Next step: email the weak sites" there is a
     ready-written email for every weak site that publishes an address,
     best prospects first, greeting the owner by name when the site
     shows it. Fill in "Email settings and template" first (your name
     and postal address are required). Read each email, then choose:
       Create Gmail drafts - puts them in Gmail > Drafts for you to send.
                             Best choice while you are getting started.
       Send now            - sends from your Gmail, one at a time, with a
                             pause of 1.5 to 4 minutes between emails.
                             Keep the app open until it finishes.
     Emailing later? Use "Email weak sites from earlier runs" and pick
     the days: most recent day, today, yesterday, this week, last week,
     last 7 / 14 / 30 days, this month, all days, one specific day or
     custom From and To dates. Businesses already emailed, or with a
     draft waiting in Gmail, are hidden unless you untick that box.

     DRAFTS YOU ARE NOT READY TO SEND: they stay in Gmail > Drafts as
     long as you like. Send them from Gmail whenever you are ready; the
     app notices on its next check, marks them sent and starts the
     4-day follow-up clock from that day. They are never drafted twice.
     If you delete a draft in Gmail instead, the app frees that
     business so it can be emailed again later.

  3. CHECK REPLIES. Every time the app opens (or when you press "Check
     Gmail for replies") it looks in your Gmail for answers and bounces.
       - Someone answered: they are marked "replied", and a reply with
         their full website breakdown and your two offers is waiting in
         Gmail > Drafts. Read it, adjust it and send it the same day.

     AUDIT REPORT ON DEMAND: in the "Audit report" box, type the
     business's serial number from the "#" column (or paste their email,
     website or name) and press Load report. The full breakdown appears:
     every problem found, most urgent first, what is already working,
     and your two offers (fix their current site, or a new AI-ready
     website you build and look after). Copy it, or press "Create Gmail
     draft reply" to put it in your Drafts as a reply in their thread.
       - Someone said "no thanks", or the address bounced: they go to the
         "Do Not Contact" tab automatically.

  4. FOLLOW UP ONCE. After 4 days without an answer the business shows
     up under "Follow-ups due". One short follow-up in the same thread,
     then it is left alone for good.

  5. RECORD THE OUTCOME. In the sheet, each business has a Status
     dropdown. Set it to "call booked", "won" or "lost" yourself. The
     app never overwrites those.

  6. READ THE SCORECARD. The "Scorecard" tab shows sites checked, weak,
     emailed, replied, reply rate, calls and wins for every niche and
     city. Keep the searches that get replies; drop the ones that do not.

YOUR TWO OFFERS
  The reply and the audit report email include two options, filled in
  with placeholder 2026 US prices so the process is complete:
    1. Fix their current website ("AI Visibility Fix", $950, 7 days)
    2. A new AI-ready website you build and look after ($2,500 to
       build, then $149 a month for hosting, updates and changes)
  Change the names, what is included, the prices, the delivery time and
  your booking link under "Email settings and template" when ready, then
  tick "Use these offers in replies". Until you tick it, replies show
  "[YOUR OFFER GOES HERE]" so a placeholder price never reaches a client.

SAFEGUARDS
    - Emails only go to addresses that belong to the business (its own
      domain, a closely related one, or a Gmail/Outlook-type mailbox).
      An address on another company's domain (often the web agency) and
      placeholder addresses like "test@" or "crud@" are never used.
    - If someone else at the business answers (for example the office
      manager from their own address), it still counts as a reply: no
      follow-up goes out, and the report reply goes to that person.
    - Out-of-office and other automatic replies are not counted as an
      answer, so the follow-up still happens.
    - Text from websites and replies can never turn into a formula in
      your sheet.
    - FAQ and service-page findings are worded "I couldn't find...",
      because the app only sees the pages it can reach.

LIMITS BUILT IN SO YOUR MAIL STAYS OUT OF SPAM
    - your daily number of emails (default 10, never more than 40),
      follow-ups included
    - a pause of 1.5 to 4 minutes between two emails
    - nobody gets a first email twice, and nobody gets more than one
      follow-up (see the "Email Log" tab)
    - anyone in the "Do Not Contact" tab is always skipped

  Read "Sending limits and how to stay out of spam" inside the app
  before your first send. Short version: start with 5 to 10 a day,
  send on weekdays, reply fast to anyone who answers, and move to a
  separate outreach domain on Google Workspace once this works.

IF YOU CONNECTED THE SHEET BEFORE THIS VERSION
  Press "Copy the connector code" in the app, paste it over the old
  code in Apps Script, then Deploy > Manage deployments > Edit >
  Version: New version. The link stays the same.
